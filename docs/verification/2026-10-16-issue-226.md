whether DNS is the sole cause of failed local connectivity; no product ICE,

## Execution decisions and their limits

- Use `.worktree/226-bot-board-pacing`, honoring the saved location preference.
  Cost: this manually placed worktree is not an app-managed attachment.
- Enable presentation runtime when a renderer link attaches, keeping pure
  engine/referee callers deterministic. Cost: a caller must attach a link to
  obtain pacing; real Board paths do so.
- Initialize the presentation phase after the initial StartGate opens, so intro
  time does not spend a decision allowance. Cost: one additional publication
  when the opening gate releases.
- Headless session tests explicitly acknowledge immediate presentation and use
  paced time budgets. Cost: those fixtures alone do not cover a slow renderer;
  dedicated delayed Board/session cases cover that boundary.
- Preserve recipient-specific resync/history when deferring returning-player
  clocks, and retain original recovery wait bounds. Cost: browser recovery
  acceptance remains part of the blocked real-connection matrix.
- Commit engine timing projection and seat rotation together because their
  shared projection was verified together. Cost: less granular cherry-picking.
- Replace two fixed test sleeps with bounded completion waits. Cost: timing
  regressions within those bounds need duration-specific checks; animation
  durations themselves are unchanged.
- The reviewer declined to judge real WebRTC acceptance without a channel. That
  criterion remains unverified, not waived; non-host symptoms might remain
  despite the passing automated and local control checks.
